# Personal Account Fee Policy

Effective 1 March 2026. This policy replaces every earlier fee list and FAQ page.

## 1. About this policy

This policy lists the fees for personal accounts. It applies to our three account plans: Standard, Plus and Premium. Where a fee differs between plans, the section says so. Where a section names no plan, the fee is the same for all three plans.

All amounts are in US dollars. A "month" is a calendar month. A "business day" is Monday to Friday, without public holidays. Fees are taken from the account on the day the service is used, unless a section says otherwise.

If this policy and another document disagree (an older fee list, an FAQ page, a marketing email), this policy applies. Support agents cannot change a fee, but they can apply the refund described in section 12.

## 2. Monthly account fees

The Standard plan has no monthly fee.

The Plus plan costs $6 per month. The fee is waived in any month in which the average daily balance is $1,500 or more.

The Premium plan costs $15 per month. The fee is waived in any month in which the average daily balance is $10,000 or more.

The monthly fee is charged on the first business day of the following month. A customer can change plan once per month, free of charge. The new plan starts on the first day of the next month.

## 3. Cash withdrawals at ATMs

Withdrawals at our own ATMs are free on every plan.

Withdrawals at another bank's ATM inside the country:

- Standard: $3 per withdrawal.
- Plus: the first 3 withdrawals per month are free, then $3 per withdrawal.
- Premium: free.

Withdrawals at an ATM in another country cost $5 per withdrawal plus 1.5% of the amount withdrawn. On the Premium plan the $5 is waived, and the 1.5% still applies. The free withdrawals of the Plus plan do not apply abroad.

The ATM operator may charge its own fee on top. That fee is shown on the ATM screen and is not ours to refund.

The daily ATM withdrawal limit is $500 on the Standard and Plus plans and $2,000 on the Premium plan. The limit counts all ATM withdrawals made between midnight and midnight, at any ATM. A customer can ask for a temporary higher limit for up to 7 days by calling support.

## 4. Card purchases

Card purchases in US dollars are free.

Card purchases in a foreign currency carry a foreign purchase fee, charged as a percentage of the purchase amount:

- Standard: 2%.
- Plus: 1%.
- Premium: no fee.

The amount is converted with the card network's exchange rate on the day the purchase is settled, which can be one or two days after the purchase. The fee is shown as a separate line on the statement.

When a purchase in a foreign currency is refunded by the shop, the foreign purchase fee for that purchase is refunded too.

## 5. Transfers

**Domestic transfers.** A standard domestic transfer is free and arrives in 1 to 2 business days. An instant transfer arrives within minutes and costs 1% of the amount, with a minimum fee of $0.50 and a maximum fee of $10.

**International transfers (wires).** An outgoing international wire costs $25 when the amount is $5,000 or less, and $40 when the amount is above $5,000. In addition, a currency conversion fee of 0.75% of the amount applies to every outgoing international wire.

- Plus: the first outgoing international wire of each month is charged half of the wire fee ($12.50 or $20). The currency conversion fee is not reduced.
- Premium: every outgoing international wire costs a flat $15, whatever the amount. The currency conversion fee still applies.

An incoming international wire costs $10 on every plan.

International wires sent before 2 p.m. on a business day leave the same day. They usually arrive in 2 to 4 business days. A wire can be cancelled free of charge until it has left the bank.

## 6. Overdrafts

An account is overdrawn when its balance is below $0.

There is a $50 buffer: if the account is overdrawn by $50 or less at the end of the day, no fee is charged for that day.

If the account is overdrawn by more than $50 at the end of the day, the overdraft fee is $20 for each item (payment, withdrawal or transfer) that was paid on that day while the account was overdrawn. No more than 3 overdraft fees are charged per day, and no more than $120 in overdraft fees per month.

A customer who brings the balance back to $0 or more by 10 p.m. on the next business day is not charged the fees for that day.

We do not charge a fee for a payment that we decline because of missing funds.

## 7. Cards: replacement and delivery

The first replacement card in a calendar year is free, whatever the reason (lost, stolen, damaged). Each further replacement card in the same calendar year costs $8.

Standard delivery is free and takes 5 to 7 business days. Express delivery takes 2 business days and costs $18 extra, also when the card itself is free.

A card that is replaced because it expires is always free and is sent 30 days before the expiry date.

A lost or stolen card must be frozen in the app or by phone as soon as the customer notices. Freezing and unfreezing a card is free.

## 8. Disputing a charge

A customer who does not recognise a charge, or was charged the wrong amount, can dispute it. A dispute must be opened within 45 days of the date of the statement on which the charge first appears. After 45 days we can still ask the merchant, but we cannot guarantee a refund.

When a dispute is opened, we put a provisional credit for the disputed amount on the account within 7 business days. The customer can use this money while we investigate.

We finish the investigation within 30 days. If the dispute is decided in the customer's favour, the provisional credit becomes final. If it is not, the provisional credit is taken back, and we tell the customer 5 business days in advance.

Opening a dispute is free. A customer can dispute several charges at once, but each charge is one dispute with its own decision.

## 9. Statements, cheques and other services

Electronic statements are free. A paper statement sent by post costs $4 per month.

A cheque paid into the account is available after 3 business days. If a cheque that a customer paid in is returned unpaid, the returned cheque fee is $12.

A stop payment on a cheque or on a recurring payment costs $15 per stop payment.

Cash deposits are free up to $5,000 per month. On the part of the month's cash deposits above $5,000, the fee is 0.5%.

A copy of a statement older than 24 months costs $5 per statement.

## 10. Inactive accounts

An account is inactive when the customer has made no payment, withdrawal, deposit or transfer for 18 months in a row. Interest and fees do not count as activity.

We write to the customer 60 days before an account becomes inactive. From the first full month after the 18 months, an inactivity fee of $5 per month is charged, until the customer uses the account again or the balance reaches $0. The inactivity fee never makes the balance negative.

Logging in to the app does not count as activity. One transaction of any size ends the inactivity.

## 11. Closing an account

Closing an account is free, with one exception: if the account is closed within 90 days of the day it was opened, an early closure fee of $25 is charged.

Before an account is closed, the balance must be $0 or more, and all pending payments must be settled. The remaining balance is sent by standard domestic transfer, free of charge, to an account the customer names.

## 12. Fee refunds and changes to this policy

Once in any 12 months, a customer can ask for a courtesy refund of one fee from this policy. A support agent can approve it without a manager when the fee is $40 or less. The courtesy refund does not cover the monthly account fee.

A fee that we charged by mistake is always refunded in full and does not count as a courtesy refund.

We announce every change to this policy at least 30 days before it takes effect, by email and in the app.
