# Final MP1 demo and coaching handoff

Use this procedure after the assessed baseline/corrected comparison is complete.
A preliminary report is not a completed submission. The live acceptance checklist
remains [P1-03 / issue #4](https://github.com/cjosewski/aia-plan/issues/4).

## Prepare the evidence

1. Follow the [analysis runbook](ANALYSIS-RUNBOOK.md) to verify the final report and
   create a new local export. Keep the code commit, `uv.lock`, reference-pack
   permission records and original source/license notices with the handoff.
2. Read `comparison.checks` and `comparability_reasons` in `report.json`. Every
   quantitative check must pass, or a specific instructor disposition must be
   retained. Do not relabel a pending report as ready.
3. Confirm that both cohorts have at least 50 retained items, 20 authentic blind
   human reviews and complete judgments from one frozen qualified judge. Preserve
   failed generation attempts, previous judge experiments and any amendments.
4. Open the five chart images and their CSV tables. Check that plotted values and
   captions match the report. Keep missing cells visible if discussing incomplete
   work; do not describe such a demonstration as a completed comparison.
5. Review source permissions before sending any package to an instructor or other
   recipient. Current local-only evidence export does not grant redistribution.

## Record a 90–180 second demonstration

Use the actual final report values in the narration. This outline takes about two
minutes; the times are targets, not a claim that a recording already exists.

| Time | Show | Explain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–20 seconds | README, schema and five category names | The engineering adaptation, seven-field contract and six-dimensional rubric |
| 20–40 seconds | Generation/gate summary | Planned initial slots, initial schema validity, retries, rejections and balanced retained counts |
| 40–65 seconds | Frozen review/qualification/calibration receipts | Authentic human review, distinct judge identity, coverage and agreement in every dimension |
| 65–100 seconds | Dimension chart and category heatmaps | Measured baseline weakness, the specific generator correction and corrected results |
| 100–120 seconds | Agreement, category distribution and overall bars | Failure reduction, denominator controls, and independent cohorts rather than paired items |
| 120–140 seconds | Verified export manifest and limitations | Reproducibility, source permissions, failed experiments and unresolved instructor decisions |

Keep credentials and unrelated desktop content out of the recording. Show saved
results rather than waiting through a model run. Record the actual duration and
file SHA-256 in the final handoff receipt; preserve the original video file.

## Coaching review and closeout

Bring the report, five charts, demo, source/adaptation decisions and reproducible
instructions to the coaching review. Record who reviewed them, the date, the
decision and any requested changes. Owner-reported assignment acceptance remains
distinct from measured completion and observed coaching approval.

Close P1-03 only after the assignment deliverables and required human acceptance
are genuinely complete. A passing software test suite, promising development
judge, or manual project-board status cannot substitute for those records.
