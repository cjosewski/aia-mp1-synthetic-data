# M4 task-fulfillment development retry

November 18, 2026. The first uncertainty development journal stopped after one
facts response and an attributed technical-audit rejection. Its ArcPy case
marked K0009 and K0026 supported by citing the task's CRS requirement, although
the candidate did not retrieve or preserve source CRS metadata and suggested
applying a geometry property to coordinate values. This is a source-applicability
failure. The original journal and completion are retained unchanged.

This new bounded trial changes **only the facts system prompt**. For task
comparisons it instructs the verifier to evaluate what the complete candidate
answer actually does, including its steps and tips. Repetition of an obligation
in the question cannot establish answer completeness. A task passage establishes
what is required; it cannot alone establish that the candidate fulfilled it.
The prompt continues to require exact citations, explicit insufficient statuses
for prepared claims, and no D1–D6 ratings. The score prompt and version 2 packet
remain byte-for-byte frozen from the prior trial.

The fixed allocation is the same ten exposed cases, two per category, at most
20 model starts, one facts request and one score request per case. Each score
requires a separate accepted fact audit. One technical-audit rejection, two
consecutive structural failures, or an unknown transport outcome stops the
trial. Every response and error is retained. It is development evidence only.

Advance only with ten complete audited pairs, at least 8/10 agreement separately
in D1–D6, more total matches than V15 on those same cases, no dimension
regression, no increase in false approvals, zero D2/D3 false approvals, and no
unsupported decisive finding. If it advances, a separately recorded assessed
extension on the original frozen twenty human reviews must still establish at
least 16/20 agreement separately in D1–D6 with complete coverage. Human labels,
historical results, and the 80% criterion remain unchanged.
